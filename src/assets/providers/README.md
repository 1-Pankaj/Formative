# Provider marks

`codex.svg` and `claude-code.svg` are unchanged copies of the provider marks distributed in Paperclip's `ui/public/brands/` directory. Their source paths and the required MIT notice are recorded in [THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md](../../../THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md).

Formative displays each mark as supplied, on a neutral dark tile for contrast. The marks identify supported providers only. OpenAI and Anthropic retain their respective names, logos, and trademarks; their presence does not imply endorsement.
