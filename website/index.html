Five things in one app

Everything your project needs.

1
Canvas

Sketch how it all fits together.

Draw a box for each part of your project and link them with lines. Move, zoom and rearrange freely. It’s saved right inside your project.

2
Code map

See what your code really looks like.

Point Formative at a folder and it draws how your files connect. Solid lines are found in your code. Dashed lines are guesses, and they’re always labelled.

3
Tasks

Keep track of the work.

Add tasks, change their status and hand them to teammates. Send a task to an AI helper and it knows exactly what you mean.

4
AI helpers

Ask your AI helpers. Safely.

Chat with Codex or Claude Code, the tools you may already use. Formative starts them in a look-but-don’t-touch mode: they can read your project, never change it.

5
The office

A tiny 3D office for your team.

Every project has its own office. Add up to 23 teammates, name them, give them roles, and watch real activity from your AI helpers appear as people hard at work.

Understands your code
Speaks the languages you write.
The code map reads your files on your own computer and shows how they connect. It never runs your code, installs anything or uploads a single file.


JavaScriptTypeScriptVueSvelteAstroPythonGoRustJavaC#PHPRuby
Next.jsRemixSvelteKitNuxtAstro routesDockerpackage.jsonPython projects


Very precise
JavaScript and TypeScript
Imports are read with a real code parser, including the scripts inside Vue, Svelte and Astro files. If a link is shown, it’s really there.
Careful guesses
Everything else
Python, Go, Rust, Java, C#, PHP and Ruby use simpler pattern matching, so a few connections may be missed. We’d rather miss one than invent one.
Knows your pages
Routes and data
It spots pages in Next.js, Remix, SvelteKit, Nuxt and Astro, and suggests links between API calls and routes. Suggestions are always labelled as guesses.


Good to know: code that’s loaded on the fly, generated code and unusual setups may not show up. The map always tells you when it hit a limit.

Privacy, in plain words
Your project stays on your computer.

  
Saved at homeProjects, diagrams, tasks and code maps live on your machine. We never upload your source files.
Shared when you chatWhen you talk to Codex or Claude Code, your message and needed details go through their service. Their fees may apply.
Optional and localOffice chat uses a small model on your device. You pick the file, and it can’t see your project.
